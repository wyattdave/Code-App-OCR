ReceiptOCR integration for Petty Cash App - 2026-09-07

User-supplied ReceiptOCR 1.0.0 was the input to this project.
The wrapper has been modified in 2026 to add photo preprocessing, multiple
OCR passes, key-field extraction, and diagnostics. The compiled JavaScript
engine and embedded English model are preserved. All original license notices
remain included in the rebuilt browser artifact.
