ReceiptOCR third-party components
================================

This inventory covers components distributed in the single browser script and
the English model distributed for rebuilding. Copyright statements and full
upstream license texts are retained in licenses/. They are also embedded into
the generated browser script so the notices accompany single-file deployment.
Do not remove the license banner when redistributing the browser script.

Build-only npm tools
--------------------

esbuild 0.28.2 is a build tool, not part of the browser OCR runtime.
Its upstream MIT license is retained in licenses/esbuild.txt. Its executable
and node_modules are not included in this repository or the release file.

Exact license-source URLs, artifact revisions, SHA-256 values, and extraction
notes are recorded in licenses/sources.json. These notices document licensing
and attribution, not a security endorsement of the historical native libraries.
